# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 6, 2026  
**Last Updated:** February 6, 2026

## Introduction

Verity Protect ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Verity Protect mobile application and website at verityprotect.com (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

By using our Service, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not access or use our Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information:**
- Name and contact information
- Email address
- Phone number
- Password (encrypted)
- Passcode/PIN (encrypted)
- Profile relationships (e.g., "caretaker," "family member")

**User Content:**
- Trusted contact lists
- Safe phrases for identity verification
- Circle member information
- Feedback on fraud alerts
- Communications with our support team

### 1.2 Call Data We Collect

**Call Metadata:**
- Caller phone numbers (inbound and outbound)
- Call timestamps, duration, and status
- Call routing decisions and outcomes
- Fraud detection scores and risk assessments

**Call Recordings and Transcripts:**
- Audio recordings of calls processed through our Service
- Speech-to-text transcriptions of call content
- Fraud analysis signals (keywords, patterns, behavioral indicators)

**Important:** We only collect call data for calls routed through your Verity Protect phone number. We do not monitor or record calls made directly from your personal device outside our Service.

### 1.3 Information Collected Automatically

**Device and Usage Data:**
- Device type, operating system, and version
- IP address and approximate location (city/state level)
- App version and diagnostic data
- Usage patterns (screens viewed, features used, session duration)
- Performance metrics and crash reports

**Cookies and Tracking Technologies:**
- Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, and analytics
- You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings

### 1.4 Information from Third Parties

**Contact Information:**
- Contact names and phone numbers (only with your explicit permission)
- Used solely to identify trusted contacts and display caller names

**Authentication Services:**
- If you sign in with Google or other OAuth providers, we receive basic profile information (name, email, profile photo)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Provide and Improve Service

- Process and route incoming calls to detect potential fraud
- Generate real-time fraud analysis and risk scores
- Provide call playback, transcripts, and activity history
- Send fraud alerts via push notifications, email, or SMS (when enabled)
- Display caller information using your contact list
- Enable family circle features for collaborative call monitoring

### 2.2 Service Communications

- Send account verification and password reset emails
- Deliver fraud alerts and suspicious call notifications
- Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
- Send important service updates and security notices

**We will never:**
- Send marketing emails without explicit consent
- Sell your call recordings or transcripts to third parties
- Use your data for advertising targeting

### 2.3 Fraud Detection and Research

- Analyze call patterns to improve fraud detection algorithms
- Train machine learning models using aggregated, anonymized data
- Research emerging scam tactics and protection methods
- Generate security insights and threat intelligence

**Data Anonymization:** Research and analytics use aggregated, de-identified data that cannot be traced back to individual users.

### 2.4 Legal and Safety Purposes

- Comply with legal obligations and law enforcement requests
- Enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements
- Protect our rights, property, and safety
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
- Investigate and respond to reported violations

- OAuth authentication services
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

All service providers are contractually required to protect your information and use it only for purposes we specify.

### 3.5 With Your Consent

We may share information for other purposes with your explicit consent, which you can revoke at any time.

**We Never:**

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

### 4.1 Data Storage

**Location:**
- Data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure within the United States
- Backups are encrypted and geographically distributed

**Security Measures:**
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Row-level security and role-based access controls
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
- Multi-factor authentication for administrative access

### 4.2 Retention Periods

**Active Profiles:**
- Call logs, recordings, and alerts: Retained indefinitely while your profile is active
- Account information: Retained while your account exists
- Usage data: Retained for up to 2 years for analytics

**Inactive Profiles:**
- Accounts inactive for 2+ years may be archived or deleted
- We will notify you before deletion if your email is still valid

**Deleted Profiles:**
- Profile data deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
- Backups purged within 90 days
- Some anonymized analytics data may be retained longer

**Legal Holds:**
- Data subject to legal proceedings may be retained beyond normal periods

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

### 5.1 Access and Portability

**View Your Data:**
- Access your profile, call logs, and settings anytime through the app

**Export Your Data:**
- Download a complete copy of your data in JSON format
- Includes account info, call metadata, transcripts, and fraud alerts
- Available through Settings → Data & Privacy → Export your data

### 5.2 Correction and Deletion

**Update Information:**
- Correct account details through app settings
- Update trusted contacts and safe phrases anytime

**Delete Data:**
- Delete individual call records through the app
- Clear all call history: Settings → Data & Privacy → Clear records
- Delete entire account: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete account

**Account Deletion Process:**
1. Requires passcode verification
2. All profile data deleted within 30 days
3. Backups purged within 90 days
4. Process is irreversible

### 5.3 Communication Preferences

**Email Alerts:**
- Configure alert frequency in app settings
- Unsubscribe links in all alert emails
- Account/security emails cannot be disabled

**Push Notifications:**
- Control notification permissions through device settings
- Manage alert types in app settings

### 5.4 Withdraw Consent

**Contacts Permission:**
- Revoke access through Settings → Data & Privacy → System permissions
- Existing contact names are removed from your account

**OAuth Connections:**
- Disconnect Google or other OAuth providers through your account settings

### 5.5 Data Requests

For assistance with privacy rights, email us at: **support@verityprotect.com**

Include:
- Your registered email address
- Description of your request
- Verification of identity (we may request additional information)

We respond to valid requests within 30 days.
