# Writer Outreach

## Goal

Get local and niche coverage that makes Verity Protect feel real, timely, and human.

## Best writer targets

- Local news reporters
- Local business and startup writers
- Consumer protection reporters
- Aging and caregiving writers
- Health and senior-living reporters
- Tech reporters who cover useful consumer apps

## Best story angles

- A 21-year-old founder built a call-safety app after a scam hit close to home.
- Families need better tools to protect older adults from scam calls.
- Verity Protect is a new App Store launch focused on screening unknown callers before they get through.

## What to avoid

- Long feature dumps
- Unsourced claims like "billions are lost every year"
- Generic "AI startup" language
- Saying the app replaces judgment, doctors, or legal advice

## Recommended pitch from your mom

### Subject options

- Story idea: 21-year-old built app to protect seniors from scam calls
- Story idea: Verity Protect helps families stop scam calls before they get through
- Story idea: new app offers a different way to protect older adults from phone scams

### Draft

To Whom It May Concern,

I’m reaching out with a story idea that may be a fit for your readers.

Phone scams targeting older adults continue to rise, and many families feel powerless because most call blockers only try to identify known spam numbers after the fact. A new app called Verity Protect was created by my 21-year-old son after his grandmother was targeted by a phone scam.

Verity Protect was built to protect vulnerable seniors from scam calls and unwanted callers while still allowing trusted people to get through. It includes trusted contacts, Safe Phrases to reduce false alarms, My Circle so family members and caregivers can monitor activity together, and Professional Lookup to help add doctors and other care-team contacts to a trusted list more easily.

What makes Verity Protect different is that it is not just another robocall filter or spam-number database. Traditional blockers depend on lists of known scam numbers, but scammers constantly change numbers to get around those systems. Verity Protect takes a more proactive approach by screening unknown callers before they ever reach the person being protected. Trusted contacts move through quickly, while unknown callers must verify themselves or leave a message for review.

That means families are not just warned after a suspicious call happens. They have a way to stop many unwanted calls earlier, while still preserving a sense of independence for parents and grandparents and giving caregivers greater peace of mind.

The app was built with a simple goal: to give families a better way to protect the people they love from phone scams without cutting them off from legitimate callers.

If you’re interested, I’d be happy to arrange a demo, share more about how the technology works, or connect you directly with the creator of the app.
