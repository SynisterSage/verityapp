# Facility One-Pager

## Headline

Protect older adults from scam calls without adding work for your staff

## Subhead

Verity Protect is a family-first call screening app that helps screen unknown callers, let trusted contacts through faster, and give families shared visibility when something looks suspicious.

## The problem

- Older adults are frequent targets for scam and impersonation calls
- Families often find out after the damage is done
- Staff do not need another platform to manage

## The solution

Verity Protect gives families a practical tool they can set up and manage themselves.

- Unknown callers are screened first
- Trusted contacts can connect more directly
- Families can review alerts and call history together
- The older adult keeps more independence with more protection

## Why this works for facilities

- Useful resident-family safety resource
- No facility dashboard
- No staff call monitoring
- No ongoing staff support burden
- Easy to include in welcome packets or family materials

## How it works

- The family sets up call forwarding to a Verity number
- Unknown callers are screened before they reach the protected line
- Trusted contacts bypass screening
- Families can review alerts, call details, and history in the app

## Best fit

- Residents with frequent scam calls
- Families worried about impersonation or money scams
- Older adults living independently but needing extra protection
