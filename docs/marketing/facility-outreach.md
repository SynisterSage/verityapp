# Facility Outreach

## Positioning

Sell Verity Protect to families with facility endorsement.

Do not pitch this as:

- A facility monitoring platform
- A staff workflow tool
- A dashboard your staff has to learn

Pitch it as:

- A resident-safety resource families can choose
- A welcome-packet recommendation
- A way to help residents and families feel safer with no operational burden on staff

## Best people to contact

- Executive Director
- Resident Services Director
- Community Relations Director
- Marketing Director
- Admissions Director

## Offer strategy

Important:

- The app currently uses App Store auto-renewable subscriptions.
- Do not promise facility discount codes until you confirm an Apple-compatible offer-code path or another compliant offer path.

Safe offers you can make now:

- Free live demo for staff
- Printed handout for resident families
- Dedicated onboarding help for referred families
- Early partner list placement on your website later

If you later confirm Apple offer codes:

- Add a limited-time partner code or trial and update this doc.

## Cold email

### Subject options

- Resource for families worried about scam calls affecting residents
- A no-burden resident safety resource for [Facility Name]
- Help families protect older adults from scam calls

### Draft

Hi [Name],

I’m reaching out because phone scams are a real concern for older adults and their families, and I think Verity Protect may be a useful resource for your residents and their loved ones.

Verity Protect is a family-first call screening app designed to help protect older adults from scam calls while still letting trusted people get through. Unlike standard robocall blockers that mostly rely on known spam-number lists, Verity Protect focuses on caller verification. Unknown callers are screened before they reach the person being protected, trusted contacts move through faster, and families can review alerts and call history together.

It also includes Safe Phrases to reduce false alarms, My Circle so family members and caregivers can monitor and coordinate together, and Professional Lookup to help add doctors and other care-team contacts to trusted lists.

That matters because scammers often rotate numbers and bypass traditional spam filters. Verity Protect gives families a more proactive way to stop suspicious calls earlier instead of only warning them after the fact.

## Objection answers

### "Our staff cannot manage another tool."

That is exactly why the product should be positioned as a family-managed safety resource, not a staff workflow.

### "Residents are not very technical."

The buyer is usually the adult child or caregiver. They manage setup. The older adult gets a calmer call experience.

### "We do not want privacy liability."

Do not offer the facility access to resident call details. Keep the relationship informational and referral-based.
