# Agency Outreach

Assumption: this file is for senior-care agencies and referral partners, not ad agencies.

## Best-fit agency types

- Home care agencies
- Geriatric care managers
- Senior placement agencies
- Area Agencies on Aging
- Elder law practices with caregiver clients
- Senior service nonprofits

## Positioning

Pitch Verity Protect as a practical family tool you can recommend when a client is worried about scam calls, suspicious callers, confusion around unknown numbers, or older adults living alone.

## Why agencies may care

- Scam calls create financial and emotional risk
- Families often ask what they can do right now
- Agencies want useful resources, not another platform
- Verity gives them something concrete to recommend

## Cold email

### Subject options

- Resource for clients worried about scam calls targeting older adults
- A simple call-safety tool for families and caregivers
- Referral resource for older-adult scam call protection

### Draft

Hi [Name],

I’m reaching out because many families working with senior-care professionals are worried about scam calls, impersonation calls, and unwanted callers reaching older adults.

Verity Protect is a family-first call screening app that helps stop suspicious calls earlier by screening unknown callers before they get through, letting trusted contacts move faster, and giving family members shared visibility into alerts and call history.

What makes it different is that it is not just another robocall blocker or spam-number filter. Traditional tools depend heavily on lists of known numbers, while Verity Protect focuses on caller verification and family review so families have a more proactive layer of protection. It also includes Safe Phrases, My Circle for caregiver and family monitoring, and Professional Lookup so trusted doctors and care-team contacts can be added more easily.

I thought it may be a useful resource for clients who ask how to reduce scam-call risk without taking away independence.

If helpful, I can send a simple overview, a printable handout, or set up a short demo.

Best,
[Name]
[Email]
[Phone]
