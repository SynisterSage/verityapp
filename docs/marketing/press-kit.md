# Press Kit

## What this is

A press kit is not the full first email.

It is the asset package you keep ready so that when a writer, editor, producer, facility, or agency asks for more, you can send one clean link instead of scrambling.

## Best way to use it

### First cold email

Keep the first email short.

Include:

- The pitch
- Website link
- App Store link
- One line offering the press kit if helpful

Use a line like:

- `If helpful, I can also send a press kit with screenshots, logo files, founder photos, and a short demo video.`

### After someone replies

Send:

- The press kit link
- The 2 or 3 most useful assets for that person
- A short note offering a demo or interview

### Do not do this

- Do not attach a giant zip to the first cold email
- Do not send 10 random screenshots with no labels
- Do not mix old SafeCall branding with Verity Protect branding
- Do not send huge raw video files by email if a share link works better

## Recommended folder structure

Use one shared folder called:

- `Verity Protect Press Kit`

Inside it, keep these folders:

- `01-founder`
- `02-brand`
- `03-product-screenshots`
- `04-demo-video`
- `05-fact-sheet`
- `06-contact`

## What to put in it

### 01-founder

- Founder headshot
- Short founder bio
- Longer founder story paragraph

### 02-brand

- App icon
- Logo PNG
- Logo with transparent background

### 03-product-screenshots

- Home screen
- Calls screen
- Alerts screen
- My Circle screen
- Trusted contacts or Doctor Lookup screen

### 04-demo-video

- 30-60 second demo video
- Optional shorter vertical version for social or TV

### 05-fact-sheet

One PDF or markdown export with:

- What Verity Protect is
- Who it is for
- What makes it different
- Website
- App Store link
- Contact info

## Core points the fact sheet should include

- Verity Protect is not just a robocall blocker or spam-number list.
- Unknown callers are screened before they get through.
- Trusted contacts can move through faster.
- My Circle lets family members and caregivers monitor together.
- Safe Phrases help reduce false alarms and add context.
- Professional Lookup helps add doctors and other care-team contacts to trusted lists.

## Minimum version to build first

If you want a fast version this week, start with just:

- Founder headshot
- App icon
- 3 screenshots
- 1 short demo video
- 1 one-page fact sheet
- Contact info

## Best workflow

1. Send the short outreach email.
2. If they reply, send the press kit link.
3. If they want coverage, send the most relevant assets directly and offer an interview or demo.
