# Family One-Pager

## Headline

Protect your parent or grandparent from scam calls without taking away independence

## Subhead

Verity Protect screens unknown callers first, lets trusted contacts through faster, and helps your family review suspicious calls together.

## Why families use it

- Scam callers often target older adults
- Suspicious calls can sound urgent and convincing
- Families want visibility without constant fear or confusion

## What Verity Protect does

- Screens unknown callers before they get through
- Lets trusted contacts connect more directly
- Gives family members shared alerts and call history
- Makes suspicious calls easier to review after they happen

## How it works

- Calls route through a Verity number first
- Unknown callers are screened
- Trusted contacts bypass the screening step
- Families can review alerts, history, and call details inside the app

## Who it is for

- Adult children helping a parent
- Families supporting a grandparent
- Caregivers helping an older adult stay safer and more independent

## Why families like it

- More protection
- More visibility
- Less confusion
- Less dependence on guessing whether a call was legitimate
