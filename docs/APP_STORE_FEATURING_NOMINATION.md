## Paste-Ready Nomination Description

Verity Protect is a family-first call screening app built to help people catch scam and spam calls before they cause harm. Instead of only blocking unknown numbers, it screens calls to a dedicated Verity number, lets trusted callers through quickly, flags suspicious activity in real time, and gives families clear call context they can act on together.

The app is especially useful for older adults, caregivers, and families who need a calmer way to manage call safety. Users can review call details, transcripts, alerts, and shared history in one place, which makes suspicious calls easier to understand and respond to without confusion.

## Paste-Ready Helpful Details

Verity Protect is live on the App Store and focuses on clarity, trust, and fast decision-making during stressful call moments. The experience is designed so protection feels readable and calm, not technical or overwhelming.

Core product strengths:
- Unknown callers are screened before they reach the user.
- Trusted callers route through quickly.
- High-risk calls are flagged with clear alerts and context.
- Families can share visibility through a circle model instead of handling call safety alone.
- Call details, transcripts, and timelines help users review what happened after the call.
