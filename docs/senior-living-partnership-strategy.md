# Senior Living Facility Partnership Strategy

## Target Market Analysis

### Who's Involved
1. **End User:** Elderly residents (60-90+)
   - Limited tech skills
   - Vulnerable to scams
   - May have cognitive decline
   
2. **Decision Maker:** Adult children (30-60)
   - More tech-savvy
   - Concerned about parent's safety
   - Willing to pay for peace of mind
   - Handle setup and monitoring
   
3. **Facility:** Assisted living / Senior communities
   - Want to differentiate their offering
   - Care about resident safety
   - Don't want operational burden
   - Limited tech resources

### Key Insight
**You're NOT selling to facilities to manage - you're selling to families WITH facility endorsement**

## Recommended Approach: Facility Partnership Program

### Why This Works
✅ Facility gets safety amenity without operational burden
✅ Families trust facility recommendation
✅ Bulk discount incentivizes adoption
✅ No complex B2B dashboard needed
✅ Facility handles zero management

### What Facilities Do
1. Include Verity Protect info in welcome packet
2. Mention during tours ("We partner with Verity Protect for scam protection")
3. Provide discount code to new residents
4. Maybe add to their website/amenities list

### What They DON'T Do
❌ Monitor calls for residents
❌ Manage accounts
❌ Provide tech support
❌ Deal with any operations

## Objection Handling

**"Our residents already have call blocking"**
→ "Traditional blockers miss 60% of scams. Our AI analyzes voice patterns in real-time, catching even new scam tactics."

**"Residents won't adopt new technology"**
→ "Their phone works exactly the same. Adult children set it up remotely - residents notice nothing except fewer scam calls."

**"This sounds complicated for our staff"**
→ "Zero involvement from your staff. Families manage everything through the app. You just provide our brochure in welcome packets."

**"What if they have questions?"**
→ "We handle all support directly with families. Your staff won't receive any calls about it."

**"Is this HIPAA compliant?"**
→ "We don't access medical information. We only analyze incoming calls. No PHI involved."

**"What's the cost to us?"**
→ "Absolutely free. No setup fees, no ongoing costs. Just share our materials with families."
